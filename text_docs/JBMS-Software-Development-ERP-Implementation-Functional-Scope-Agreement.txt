2. Project Overview
The Developer shall design, develop, configure, test, deploy, and assist in the implementation of the JAPA Business Management System (JBMS).
JBMS shall be developed as an integrated Enterprise Resource Planning and Business Management System supporting multiple stores or branches, multiple production areas or production sites, centralized management visibility, store-specific operations, production-area-specific operations, operational financial management, executive analytics, and future business expansion.
The initial integrated scope includes the following major functional areas:
• Customers and CRM
• Quotations and Pricing
• Sales Orders
• Job Orders
• Multi-Production Management
• Inventory and Inventory Valuation
• Purchasing and Suppliers
• Collections and Accounts Receivable
• Expenses and Operational Financial Management

• Costing and Profitability
• Employee Management
• Attendance and Payroll
• Reports, Dashboards and Business Analytics
3. Primary Business Objective
The primary purpose of JBMS is not only to digitize transactions, but to reduce the amount of time the Business Owner and management spend manually checking daily operations.


The system is intended to provide timely visibility into:
• Sales and collections
• Available recorded funds
• Customer receivables and overdue balances
• Purchasing requirements and supplier obligations
• Inventory valuation, low stock and restocking needs
• Slow-moving and non-moving inventory
• Job Order progress and production workload
• At-risk and delayed production
• Upcoming deadlines and material shortages
• Product, service, customer, store and production-area performance
• Operational profitability and management exceptions
The intended result is to allow management to devote more time to marketing, customer acquisition, business development, strategic planning, expansion, and customer relationships rather than continuous manual operational monitoring.
4. Business Structure
4.1 Multiple Stores / Branches
Each approved store or branch may maintain its own operational records, subject to authorized access and the approved configuration.
• Sales and Sales Orders
• Customers and collections
• Expenses
• Inventory and stock movements
• Users and permissions
• Store-level reports and analytics
Management shall also have access to consolidated information across approved stores.
4.2 Multiple Production Areas / Sites
JBMS shall support more than one production area, production department, or production site. Each production area may maintain its own operational queue and performance data.
• Assigned Job Orders
• Production schedule and queue
• Employees and teams
• Machines where applicable
• Production stages and work-in-progress
• Material requirements and consumption

• Delays, quality control and completion records
• Production-area reports and analytics
Production areas may represent different physical locations, departments, or specialized processes such as design, sublimation printing, DTF, cutting, heat press, sewing, embroidery, finishing, quality control, and packing.

6. Sales Order and Job Order Responsibilities
6.1 Sales Order
Sales Order represents what was sold to the customer. It may include printing supplies, merchandise, printing services, customized products, and other approved items. Sales Order shall not directly control detailed production stages.
6.2 Job Order
Job Order represents what must be produced or performed. A Sales Order requiring production may create or reference one or more Job Orders. A Job Order may be assigned to one production area, move through multiple areas, be partially completed, or generate reprint/replacement work where applicable.
